IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
- Play Balcade_Pong_60s_Master.wav or .mp3 as cinematic.
- On completion at 60s, start Balcade_Pong_Menu_Seamless_Loop.wav or .ogg at 0.
- The final 3 seconds of the cinematic contain the loop's last 3 seconds,
  fading up while the original song fades down, ready for sample-continuous
  replay from the loop's beginning.
- Loop length: 13.7142857 seconds (8 bars at 140 BPM), no programmed fade.
- Use Web Audio AudioBufferSourceNode.loop for gapless browser playback.
- Fade menu music using GainNode automation when disabled in settings.
- The WAV music and SFX stems sum to the cinematic master (minor 16-bit quantisation aside).
- All added effects were generated using synthesis/noise; no third-party sound samples.
- Preview is a single file of cinematic plus two repeats, for evaluating the seam.
