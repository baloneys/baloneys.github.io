IMPLEMENTATION
- Cinematic mix is exactly 60.000 seconds, 44.1 kHz stereo.
- The cinematic MIX already previews the first 2.0 seconds of the loop at 58–60. To avoid a jump at 60, either start the menu loop at 58s and crossfade away the cinematic, or at 60s continue the menu loop from +2.0 seconds instead of restarting at 0.
- In implementation, don't stack the MIX with SFX or MUSIC stems: the MIX already includes both.
- If the engine can only start menu audio from zero at the 60s handover, use a short ~0.5s crossfade to mask the restart; for best results use the offset method above.
- Adjust the cut or SFX timing if shot lengths change.
- All extra Mac-like alert and startup sounds are ORIGINAL SYNTHESIZED EFFECTS (not recordings of Apple's proprietary sounds).
